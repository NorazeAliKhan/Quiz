Born from the Pursuit of the Ultimate Acoustic Thock

Founded in 2021 by a team of mechanical engineers and software developers dissatisfied with hollow plastic mainstream keyboards, KeyForge was created to treat mechanical keyboards as heirloom desktop instruments.

Every board is crafted from solid aircraft-grade 6063 aluminum, precision CNC-machined over 4 hours, and hand-finished with anodized micro-bead blasting. We damp each unit with five acoustic Poron gasket layers and hand-lubricate every switch with authentic Krytox 205g0.
